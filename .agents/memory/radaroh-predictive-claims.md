---
name: RadarOH predictive claims
description: Guardrails for scores, trends, recommendations, and future predictive automation.
---

RadarOH must label current competitor scores and trend directions as deterministic historical signals, not calibrated forecasts. Recommendations remain review-only and must cite persisted evidence.

**Why:** The platform has evidence and event history, but no labeled outcomes, intervention records, or human feedback loop from which to measure forecast calibration, recommendation value, or causal impact.

**How to apply:** Keep score formulas transparent, compare equivalent historical periods, cite real event IDs, and require human review. Introduce predictive probability or automatic execution only after outcome and feedback data can support evaluation.
